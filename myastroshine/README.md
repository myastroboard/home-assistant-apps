# Home Assistant App: MyAstroShine

Turn your astrophotos into finished images, in your browser.

![Supports aarch64 Architecture][aarch64-shield]
![Supports amd64 Architecture][amd64-shield]

- **One photo** - Auto Astro sets everything in one click, then fine-tune step by step
- **Several shots of the same target** - stacking with calibration frames, quality scoring and drizzle
- **Clean the sky** - gradient and light-pollution removal, denoise, star removal
- **3D effect** - depth-shift animations from a single image
- **Astrodex** - open a photo from MyAstroBoard, send the result back
- **Optional ML engines** - StarNet2 star removal and DeepSNR denoise (amd64)

See the **Documentation** tab to get started.
